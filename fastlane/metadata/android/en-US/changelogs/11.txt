• System › new AI analysis screen. One read-only sweep of the router — board, load, storage, wireless and network config, DHCP leases, wifi clients, recent log warnings — is redacted on the phone (Wi-Fi keys and passwords never leave it), shown in full before anything is sent, and then streamed to any OpenAI-compatible chat endpoint you configure: OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq or your own. The provider's base URL, model and API key live in the AI provider card; the key is sealed with the phone's keystore like a router password. An optional question focuses the analysis; the answer arrives as VERDICT / FINDINGS / ADVICE and can be copied. Nothing is sent anywhere until you press Analyze.
