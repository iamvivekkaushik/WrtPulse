• Mesh: Network › Mesh turns on seamless hand-off (802.11r/k/v) and makes your other OpenWrt routers nodes of this one, over a cable or 802.11s. A backup is saved first; Leave mesh restores it.

• LEDs: System › LEDs sets each LED's mode and colour, with an optional connectivity watch.
